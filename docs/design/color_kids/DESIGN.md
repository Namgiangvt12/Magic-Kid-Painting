## Brand & Style

The visual identity is anchored in **Tactile Neo-Playfulness**: an intuitive, joyful, and sensory-rich digital coloring playground built expressly for children aged 3 to 8 and trusted by parents.

### Core Principles
- **Effortless Agency & Delight:** Big physical affordances invite hands-on exploration. Every surface suggests squishy touchability, tactile depth, and immediate physical cause-and-effect.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Visual signifiers, rich silhouettes, and vocal/haptic cues lead the experience over dense written copy to accommodate pre-readers and emergent readers.
- **Calm Warmth over Chaos:** Unlike over-stimulating arcade games, the aesthetic utilizes warm, creamy backgrounds with soft vibrant pastels to foster focused, soothing artistic play.
- **Rigorous Safety by Separation:** The child-facing sandbox and the adult administrative ecosystem exist in distinct design modes. The child UI is entirely gesture-driven, chunky, and insulated; the Parent Zone is grounded, tranquil, and secured behind deliberate physical parental gates compliant with COPPA guidelines.

## Colors

The palette balances cheerful stimulation with long-session visual comfort, relying on an organic, buttery warm surface instead of harsh clinical whites.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#38BDF8` Sky Blue):** Main navigation anchors, primary action buttons (brush selection, forward/play triggers), and primary active borders.
- **Secondary (`#A78BFA` Soft Pastel Purple):** Magic wand tools, special effects, secondary modes, and transition states. Supported by accent purple `#C084FC`.
- **Tertiary (`#FBBF24` Sunny Warm Yellow):** Trophies, star counters, reward bursts, and celebration highlights. Paired with warm amber `#F59E0B` for button bottom-bevels.
- **Accents:**
  - **Mint Green (`#34D399`):** Affirmative actions, "Save/Done", audio toggles, and safe progress indicators.
  - **Bright Coral / Warm Orange (`#FB7185` / `#FB923C`):** Eraser, clear canvas tool, undo prompts, and attention badges.
- **Canvas & Surface Neutral (`#FFFDF7` Soft Butter):** Base art paper texture and background container layer. Off-canvas tool trays and parent headers utilize `#F8FAFC`.
- **Text & Outlines:**
  - **Primary Ink (`#1E293B` Deep Blueberry):** Softened deep blue-grey for ultra-clear line work, chunky outlines, and text. Never use pure black (`#000000`).
  - **Muted Label Ink (`#64748B`):** Descriptive metadata and parent gate challenge text.
- **Rainbow Crayon Palette:** A standardized 12-color saturated crayon tier (`#EF4444`, `#F97316`, `#FBBF24`, `#10B981`, `#06B6D4`, `#3B82F6`, `#6366F1`, `#8B5CF6`, `#EC4899`, `#F43F5E`, `#78350F`, `#1E293B`) rendered inside physical crayon wrappers.

## Typography

The type system blends the bubble-like charm of **Quicksand** for headlines and interactive labels with the readability and soft curves of **Nunito Sans** for descriptive copy and parent documentation.

### Usage Directives
- **Pre-reader Readability:** Text is kept minimal in child-facing canvas screens. Use bold, expressive, title-cased labels paired with distinct graphical iconography.
- **Kerning and Spacing:** Rounded typefaces risk visual collisions at heavy weights; generous tracking and tall x-heights maintain legibility from viewing distances common with propped-up tablets.
- **Parent Gate Typographic Contrast:** Shift away from display sizes to regular `body-md` and `headline-sm` inside the adult dashboard to establish immediate tonal boundaries.

## Layout & Spacing

Color Kids employs a flexible full-viewport fluid layout designed for landscape tablet orientation (the primary canvas viewport for children) with seamless reflow for vertical mobile handoffs.

### Hit Targets and Finger Spacing
- **Minimum Target Rule:** Child interaction nodes strictly enforce an absolute minimum dimension of **56px × 56px**, expanding to **72px to 88px** for core canvas tools (brushes, undo, home).
- **Accidental Tap Buffers:** Outer screen borders preserve a minimum safe gutter of `1.25rem` (20px) to prevent wrist/palm resting triggers on edge bezel devices.
- **Layout Organization:**
  - **Canvas Workspace:** Pinned center stage with flexible aspect ratios, preserved inside a butter-cream framing card.
  - **Tool Palettes:** Floating rounded dock docked to either bottom or side edge depending on orientation.
  - **Top Utility Shelf:** Level progress, undo/redo, and parent gate triggers isolated to top screen corners with generous separation (`space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a tactile **3D Toy Box extrusion** style, avoiding realistic skeuomorphism or flat abstraction in favor of pill-like, chunky objects that press into the screen.

### Dimensional Mechanics
- **The "3D Toy Push":** Buttons feature a solid bottom edge border (3px to 5px solid tint) paired with a warm drop shadow.
  - *Standard State:* Drop shadow `0 6px 0px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`, plus bottom border 4px darker than element face.
  - *Pressed / Active State:* The element translates 4px down (`translateY(4px)`), bottom border collapses to 1px, and drop shadow flattens.
- **Outlines and Separation:** Interactive child components feature a 2.5px to 3.5px solid border (`#1E293B` or a deeply saturated shade of the base hue) to guarantee WCAG AAA visibility against any colored background.
- **Layer Stacking Hierarchy:**
  - **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Butter-cream ground (`#FFFDF7`). Flat with zero elevation.
  - **Layer 1 (Art Board Frame):** Recessed inset shadow (`inset 0 4px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`) with 4px border.
  - **Layer 2 (Tool Trays & Palettes):** Floating docks with soft diffused spread: `0 8px 24px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.
  - **Layer 3 (Buttons & Interactive Tokens):** Physical tactile toy shadow.
  - **Layer 4 (Modals, Parent Gate & Stickers):** High-level overlay featuring strong dimming backdrop (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.45)`) and bouncy zoom-in entry.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Super-Pill** aesthetic (Roundedness level `3`). Sharp corners do not exist anywhere within child touchpoints.

### Shape Directives
- **Pill Elements:** All action triggers, tool pickers, and color chips have fully rounded ends (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Sheets and Frames:** Canvas panels, dialog sheets, and modal cards use hyper-soft curves (`2rem` / `32px` on tablets, `1.5rem` / `24px` on mobile).
- **Ornamental Accents:** Star cutouts, bouncy dialogue callouts, and sparkle icons adopt curved inner apexes to prevent visual sharpness.

## Components

### Chunky Action Buttons
- **Style:** Pill-shaped, minimum 64px height. Thick 3px border in darker shade of fill color.
- **Bevel Foot:** 4px to 6px solid bottom edge creating a physical toy button effect.
- **Interaction:** Spring-loaded physics on release (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`), squishing inward to 92% scale on tap before popping back. Accompanied by haptic click feedback.

### Color Swatches & Crayon Tools
- **Physical Metaphor:** Crayons rendered vertically in an open wooden/pastel tray.
- **Active State:** Selected crayon bounces up 16px from the tray, displaying a sparkling star badge and subtle pulsing glow.
- **Diameter:** Swatch circles are minimum 56px across, with a 3px white inner ring and a thick outer contour matching the crayon pigment.

### Canvas Toolbars (Brushes, Eraser, Fill)
- **Container:** Rounded pill dock with a semi-opaque background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), surrounded by a 3px `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Tool Spacing:** Generous `space-sm` separation between chunky circular icon toggles to prevent fat-finger mis-taps.

### Cards & Artwork Templates
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` card with soft 24px border radius, resting on `#FFFDF7` base.
- **Preview Area:** Inset preview with light grey framing border.
- **Metadata:** Simplified star rating / progress badge in Sunny Yellow; zero unnecessary textual metadata for child profiles.

### Parent Gate
- **Purpose:** Strict barrier separating commercial, account, external link, or purchase screens from curious toddlers.
- **Design Pattern:** Explicit adult-challenge modal. Styled intentionally plain and functional compared to the colorful game UI (clean slate grey backgrounds, standard non-gamified system typography).
- **Verification Mechanics:** Multi-step cognitive locks (e.g., "Triple-tap the blue square, then drag the triangle to the circle" or mathematical phrase spelled in words: *"Nine minus four equals..."*).
- **Cooldowns:** 3 incorrect attempts trigger a 60-second cooldown timer.

### Child-Safe Ad & Reward Containers
- **Non-Intrusive Banner Policy:** No interactive, fast-flashing, or accidental-click banner placements anywhere inside the coloring canvas viewports.
- **Reward Ad Prompt:** Distinct opt-in model unlocked solely through child-friendly metaphorical framing ("Watch a cartoon to unlock the Dinosaur pack!"). Always requires confirmation and displays a prominent, high-contrast, chunky countdown exit icon.

### Form Inputs & Settings (Parent Dashboard Only)
- **Inputs:** Standard rounded-lg (`16px`) fields with high-contrast text, clear helper labels, large toggle switches, and explicit visual validation states.
